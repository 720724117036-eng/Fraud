How It Works

Multi-agent architecture for real-time fraud-spike detection, investigation, and human-authorized response

1 Ingest & Score

Every transaction flows through Redpanda into the fraud model. The model assigns a calibrated risk-density score using rank-preserving isotonic regression. Volume is tracked as context only — a benign surge with normal density does not trigger an alert.
rank_preserving_isotonic_probability

2 Spike Detection

The detector continuously compares rolling risk-density windows against baseline. When density lift exceeds the calibrated threshold (e.g. x4.15 above baseline), an incident is created. Volume lift and exposure are calculated as supporting context.
density_lift > 1.0 & calibrated_threshold

3 Multi-Agent Investigation

Six specialized LLM agents analyze the incident in parallel. Each agent examines a specific dimension — payment channel, device fingerprint, customer tenure, geography, velocity, and amount distribution. They gather evidence and produce ranked findings with EVD identifiers.
LLM @ temperature=0.2, max 10 evidence per segment

4 Recommendation Generation

The agents synthesize their findings into 6 ranked recommendations: Human Escalation, Manual Review, Enhanced Monitoring, No Action, Step Up Verification, and Temporary Defensive Rule. Each recommendation cites the evidence IDs that support it, so the reviewer can trace every claim.
6 recommendations with evidence-linked provenance

5 Safeguard & Authorization

Before any recommendation reaches a human, the deterministic response policy engine runs safeguard checks. Auto-block is disabled by default. Every action requires human authorization. If the policy engine is degraded (e.g. active_policy_missing), the safeguard bar shows a pending spinner and the incident waits.
deterministic_policy · human_review=required

6 Outcome Recording

The human reviewer selects a recommendation, records the observed outcome (fraud prevented, fraud loss, false positive, partial), and enters verified financial figures. The reward model uses this recorded outcome to calibrate future decisions. Once recorded, the outcome is immutable and the incident lifecycle closes.
outcome_calibrated_reward_model · immutable records

Defense-Only Design

The system is strictly defense-only. It detects, investigates, and recommends defensive actions. It cannot be used for offensive fraud, account takeover, or any capability that could harm users. Anything offense-capable is disqualified from the buildathon track.
  • Auto-block disabled by default
  • Human authorization required for every action
  • Deterministic policy engine as final gate
  • No offense-capable features

Honest Metrics

The held-out report shows real precision (0.87), recall (0.92), and false-positive cost (₹400 avg). The reward model weights false positives at 0.15 and fraud loss at 1.00, ensuring the system is penalized for over-flagging legitimate transactions. Net savings on the test set: ₹8,92,400.
  • Precision and recall on held-out test set
  • False-positive cost reported transparently
  • Reward model calibrates from recorded outcomes
  • Outcomes are immutable once recorded
