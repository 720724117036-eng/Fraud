5-MINUTE STORY

From transaction to trusted decision.

Automated screening + explainable evidence + human judgment.

01

Incoming transaction

Amount, hour, IP risk, device risk, failed attempts and account age enter the stream.

02

AI scoring

The trained model estimates fraud probability. If a compatible fraud_model.pkl is present, it is used automatically.

03

Explainable signals

The system translates observable features into human-readable risk factors instead of exposing only a mysterious score.

04

Automatic routing

Low-risk records are cleared, medium-risk records are watched, and high-risk records are automatically routed to incidents.

05

Human decision

An analyst reviews the evidence and can confirm fraud, mark a false positive, accept a legitimate transaction, or record uncertainty.

06

Closed-loop evidence

Completed incidents remain visible in Cleared / Closed, creating an auditable review trail for the demo.

Real public data — presentation rule

For the final presentation, use a licensed public fraud dataset and name its source, license and preprocessing steps. The built-in replay records are demo records; they must not be presented as real customer transactions.

“FraudPulse replays transaction-shaped data, scores each record, explains the risk context, routes suspicious cases, and leaves the final outcome to a human reviewer.”
